**HandPuzzle Pro – AI-Powered AR Puzzle Game**  **HandPuzzle Pro** is an interactive browser-based **Augmented Reality (AR) puzzle game** that uses **MediaPipe AI** for real-time hand tracking, enabling users to play entirely through hand gestures without a mouse or touchscreen.  Players begin by creating a **"Director's Frame"** gesture with their thumbs and index fingers to select a rectangular area in the camera view. Opening all fingers acts as a virtual camera shutter, capturing and automatically cropping the selected region while removing the user's hands from the image.  The captured image is instantly divided into puzzle pieces with selectable difficulty levels: **Easy (3×3), Medium (4×4), and Hard (5×5)**. The puzzle pieces are displayed over the live camera feed, creating an immersive AR gameplay experience.  Users solve the puzzle using intuitive **pinch-and-drag hand gestures** to grab, move, and swap puzzle tiles. Correctly placed tiles automatically lock into position with visual and audio feedback. The game also includes a **timer, move counter, scoring system, three-star rating**, and a **confetti celebration** upon successful completion.  Built using **Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and MediaPipe AI**, HandPuzzle Pro delivers a smooth, lightweight, and highly interactive AR gaming experience without relying on heavy frameworks.  **Technologies Used:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, MediaPipe AI, WebRTC (Webcam API).
